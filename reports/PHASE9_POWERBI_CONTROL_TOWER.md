## 1. Business Purpose

The NOVAMART Supply Chain Control Tower is an interactive Power BI reporting solution designed for supply chain managers, demand planners, inventory planners, and procurement analysts.

Its objective is to support the decision-making workflow:

**Monitor → Identify Risk → Prioritize → Act**

The dashboard integrates:

- Historical retail sales and inventory observations
- A 14-day Ridge Regression sales forecast
- Scenario-based inventory replenishment recommendations
- ABC inventory classification
- Simulated supplier and procurement performance
- Rule-based supply chain management priorities

The operational decision unit is the **local SKU**, representing a product at a specific retail location.

Historical sales and forecast tables retain daily granularity. Current inventory recommendations and management-priority classifications contain one row per local SKU.

The dashboard supports analytical review rather than automated operational execution.

### Page 2 — Demand Planning

**Business question:** What sales are expected, and how reliable are the forecasts?

Historical actuals and forward forecasts are displayed as distinct analytical periods.

The model predicts recorded synthetic units sold rather than verified unconstrained customer demand.

## 3. Power BI Data Model

The dashboard uses a star-style relational data model.

The prepared data package is stored in:

`dashboard/data/`

The package contains:

- 13 byte-identical upstream analytical files
- One newly generated 774-day calendar table

**Total: 14 Power BI input CSV files.**

The relational model is documented in:

`dashboard/POWERBI_MODEL.md`

Major relationships include:

- Location dimension → Local SKU dimension
- Local SKU dimension → Historical and analytical fact tables
- Date dimension → Dated historical, forecast, procurement, and evaluation facts
- Supplier dimension → Simulated procurement and supplier-related analytical tables

Single-direction relationships are used where specified to avoid ambiguous filter propagation.

The model maintains appropriate granularity across daily historical observations, daily forecasts, supplier-level scores, and SKU-level inventory recommendations.

## 4. Power BI Measures and Interactions

The dashboard uses DAX measures and interactive report filtering.

DAX definitions are documented in:

`dashboard/DAX_MEASURES.md`

The complete implementation instructions are available in:

`dashboard/POWERBI_BUILD_GUIDE.md`

### Filtering Behavior

Location, category, and SKU slicers filter analytical records through the local SKU dimension.

Supplier filtering applies to supplier-related procurement, scorecard, and current supplier-linked data.

Supplier-level scores cannot be interpreted as location-specific supplier performance without recomputing the underlying receipt-level measures.

Date filters apply to dated facts.

Fixed inventory snapshots, ABC classifications, and management-priority outputs should not be treated as historical time-series measures.

Historical sales and forecast windows remain analytically distinct.

## 6. Data Provenance and Analytical Limitations

### Published Synthetic Retail Data

The historical retail dataset is publisher-generated synthetic data.

It does not represent verified real-world business transactions.

### Demand Forecasting

The Ridge Regression model predicts historical-style units sold.

The 14-day forecast covers January 31 through February 13, 2024.

It is not a live forecast.

### Inventory Planning

Reorder points, safety stock, target inventory, and proposed order quantities are based on documented assumptions.

The calculations use a historical source inventory snapshot and simulated supplier lead times.

Verified open purchase orders, allocations, backorders, purchasing constraints, and inventory reconciliation are not included.

### Simulated Procurement

Suppliers, purchase orders, and receipts were generated for the project scenario.

They do not represent actual vendor transactions.

### ABC Classification

ABC classification is based on the 2023 synthetic historical sales-value proxy:

`SUM(units_sold × listed_price)`

It is not audited revenue, realized margin, or inventory carrying value.

### Supplier Scores

Supplier performance scores use relative min-max normalization and assumed weights.

They do not represent externally validated supplier ratings.

### Management Priorities

Critical, High, Medium, and Routine classifications are determined using documented business rules.

They identify analytical review candidates rather than confirmed business incidents.

### Business Impact

No purchase orders were executed through the project.

No realized cost savings, service-level improvements, or financial returns are claimed.

## 7. Example Management Workflow

A supply chain planner can use the dashboard to:

1. Open the Executive Overview to identify major inventory exceptions.
2. Navigate to the Supply Chain Action Center.
3. Filter management priority to Critical.
4. Review Class A local SKUs at a selected location.
5. Inspect current inventory against the scenario reorder point.
6. Examine the 14-day forecast and proposed replenishment quantity.
7. Review the simulated primary supplier's performance tier.
8. Investigate the case with procurement before deciding whether to create an actual purchase order.

This workflow illustrates how the dashboard supports decision-making without automating purchases.
